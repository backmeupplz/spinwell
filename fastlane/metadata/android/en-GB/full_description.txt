Spinwell is a Navidrome player for Android: a small, fast, good-looking app with no ads that streams your own music collection from your server and downloads it for offline listening. It works with Navidrome and with any other server that speaks the OpenSubsonic or Subsonic API. Sign in with your server address, username and password, and your albums, artists and playlists are right there.

LISTEN
• Stream songs in their original quality, FLAC included, or choose a lower quality for mobile data
• Lock screen, notification, headset and Bluetooth controls
• Synced lyrics that follow the song, and a tap on a line jumps to it
• Smart shuffle that keeps the same artist from playing twice in a row
• An Up next list you can reorder, with Play next and Add to queue on every song
• Picks up where you left off: your song, your queue and your place in the track

YOUR LIBRARY
• A home screen with recently played, recently added, most played and favourite albums
• Artists, albums, playlists, songs and favourites, with big sharp covers
• Search that finds artists, albums and songs while you type, ignoring case and accents
• Favourites that sync with your server
• Your plays are reported to your server, so play counts and history stay right; you can switch this off

OFFLINE
• Download a song, an album, a playlist or all of an artist's songs; an interrupted download continues where it stopped
• Keep the original files or save smaller MP3s
• When your server can't be reached, for example with Tailscale switched off, Spinwell switches to your downloads by itself and catches up once you're back
• See and remove all downloads in one place

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Spinwell only talks to the server you sign in to. No accounts with us, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app is free and open source.

Spinwell needs a Navidrome or OpenSubsonic server that you or someone you know runs. Spinwell is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Navidrome project.
