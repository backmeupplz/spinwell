Your Navidrome library, played beautifully.

Spinwell is a small, fast player for Android and iPhone that connects to your own Navidrome or OpenSubsonic server, so the music you collected sounds and looks the way it deserves, at home or with no signal at all.

Spinwell is on Google Play now, and the iPhone app is waiting for App Store review, so its link will appear here as soon as Apple approves it. It's free and open source, with no ads and no analytics.

Everything you want from a music app, and nothing you don't.

Spinwell does a few things and does them carefully, with smooth transitions, big sharp covers and a player that opens instantly.

Your whole library at hand

Browse artists, albums, playlists, songs and favorites, with recently played, recently added and most played albums waiting on the home screen.

Downloads that survive anything

Save an album, a playlist or a single song for offline listening, and an interrupted download picks up where it stopped instead of starting over.

Offline without thinking about it

When your server can't be reached, for example with Tailscale switched off, Spinwell quietly switches to what you've downloaded and catches up once you're back.

An up-next list you can shape

See what's coming, drag songs into the order you like, and add anything to play next without losing the album you're listening to.

Shuffle that feels right

Shuffle spreads your artists and albums apart so you don't hear the same band twice in a row, and turning it off returns you to the original order.

Right where you left off

Open the app and your song, your queue and your place in the track are already there, ready to continue with a single tap.

Questions people ask

What do I need to use Spinwell?

You need a Navidrome server, or any other server that speaks the Subsonic or OpenSubsonic API, plus its address, your username and your password. Spinwell talks only to that server.

Does it work with my server on Tailscale?

Yes. Enter your server's tailnet address, and Spinwell plays from it whenever your phone is on the tailnet, then falls back to your downloads when it isn't.

Will lossless files play?

FLAC plays as it is on your server by default, and you can choose a lower streaming quality for mobile data so the server converts songs to a smaller size on the fly.

Is Spinwell an official Navidrome app?

No. Spinwell is an independent open-source app made by Nikita Kolmogorov, and it isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Navidrome project.
