Privacy policy

Spinwell doesn't collect, store or share any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

The app connects only to the Navidrome or OpenSubsonic server whose address you enter. Your server address, username and a sign-in token derived from your password are stored on your device (in the Keychain on iPhone), and your password itself is never stored. Covers, library pages and downloaded songs are kept on your device so the app can work offline, and you can remove them in Settings at any time.

When "Report plays to server" is on, the app tells your own server which songs you played, so your server's play counts and history stay accurate. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.

Questions: open an issue.
